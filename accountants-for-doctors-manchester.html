Specialist Accountancy for Doctors

Whether you are employed by the NHS, working as a locum, holding a hospital consultant post or managing both NHS and private income, your tax affairs require specialist understanding. Professional Expense Claims for Doctors

Doctors can claim a range of professional expenses against their income, reducing their tax liability. HMRC's guidance on tax relief for employees covers allowable expenses for employed individuals. Self-employed locums can claim a broader range of business expenses under HMRC's self-employment expense rules.

Commonly claimable professional expenses for doctors include:

GMC annual retention fee
Royal College membership subscriptions
BMA membership (employed doctors)
Medical indemnity premiums (MDU, MPS, MDDUS)
CPD courses and medical conferences
Medical equipment and instruments used for work
Business mileage at HMRC approved rates
Examination and assessment fees

Backdated claims can be made for up to four tax years. For hospital doctors who have completed foundation and specialty training without claiming professional subscriptions, this can amount to a significant rebate. We provide a comprehensive professional expense checklist and handle the full claims process with HMRC.

NHS Pension Annual Allowance

The NHS Pension Scheme is a defined benefit arrangement. The value of pension savings for annual allowance purposes is based on the growth in the defined benefit during the year, multiplied by a factor of 16 (plus any money purchase pensions). For senior doctors with significant salary growth, this can result in an annual allowance charge. For 2026/27, HMRC's standard annual allowance is £60,000, subject to tapering for higher-income individuals.
